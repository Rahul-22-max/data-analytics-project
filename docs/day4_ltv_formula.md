# Day 4 Initial LTV Formula

## Objective

Create a first version of Customer Lifetime Value.

## Formula

LTV = MonthlyCharges × tenure

## Reasoning

MonthlyCharges:
- Monthly revenue from customer

Tenure:
- Number of months customer has remained active

Multiplying them estimates total customer value.
