# Day 3 LTV Target Selection

## Evaluated Targets

### 1. TotalCharges

Historical revenue generated by customer.

Pros:
- Easy to model
- Real data

Cons:
- Not future value

---

### 2. FutureRevenue

MonthlyCharges × Future Months

Pros:
- Forward looking

Cons:
- Unrealistic assumptions

---

### 3. Enhanced LTV

MonthlyCharges × Tenure × Retention Probability

Pros:
- Includes customer lifespan
- Includes churn risk
- Closest to business reality

## Final Decision

Target Variable:
EnhancedLTV

Reason:
Provides a more realistic representation of customer value and aligns with churn prediction objectives.

EnhancedLTV

MonthlyCharges

Benefits:

This target is more impressive on a resume than simply predicting TotalCharges.
