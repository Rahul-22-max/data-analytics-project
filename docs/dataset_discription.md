# Dataset Description

## Dataset Name

Customer Churn Dataset (Telco Customer Churn)

## Project

Customer Churn Prediction & LTV Engine

## Dataset Overview

This dataset contains information about customers of a telecommunications company. The data is used to analyze customer behavior, predict customer churn, and estimate Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

Customer churn refers to customers who stop using the company's services. By analyzing customer information and service usage patterns, businesses can identify customers at risk of leaving and take actions to improve retention.

## Dataset Size

* Rows: 7,043
* Columns: 21

## Target Variable

### Churn

Indicates whether a customer left the company.

* Yes = Customer churned
* No = Customer retained

## Important Features

### customerID

Unique identifier for each customer.

### gender

Customer gender (Male/Female).

### SeniorCitizen

Indicates whether the customer is a senior citizen.

* 1 = Yes
* 0 = No

### Partner

Whether the customer has a partner.

### Dependents

Whether the customer has dependents.

### tenure

Number of months the customer has stayed with the company.

### PhoneService

Whether the customer subscribes to phone service.

### InternetService

Type of internet service used by the customer.

### Contract

Customer contract type:

* Month-to-Month
* One Year
* Two Year

### PaymentMethod

Method used for bill payments.

### MonthlyCharges

Amount charged to the customer each month.

### TotalCharges

Total amount paid by the customer during their subscription period.

## Project Goals

1. Analyze factors influencing customer churn.
2. Build machine learning models to predict churn.
3. Identify high-risk customers.
4. Estimate Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
5. Generate business recommendations based on insights.

## Data Quality Checks

The dataset will be checked for:

* Missing values
* Duplicate records
* Incorrect data types
* Outliers
* Data consistency

## Expected Outcome

The final output of this project will be:

* Customer churn prediction model
* Customer segmentation insights
* LTV estimation
* Business recommendations for customer retention
