# Day 4 Business Insights

## 1. Churn Distribution

- Total customers: 7043
- Customers who stayed: 5174
- Customers who churned: 1869
- Overall churn rate is approximately 26.5%.

---

## 2. Churn Rate by Gender

| Gender | Churn Rate |
|--------|-----------|
| Female | 26.92% |
| Male | 26.16% |

### Insight

- Churn rate is almost the same for males and females.
- Gender is not a major factor affecting churn.

---

## 3. Churn Rate by Contract Type

| Contract Type | Churn Rate |
|--------------|-----------|
| One Year | 11.27% |
| Two Year | 2.83% |

### Insight

- Customers with long-term contracts churn less.
- Two-year contracts have the lowest churn rate.
- Businesses should encourage customers to choose long-term contracts.

---

## 4. Churn Rate by Tenure Group

| Tenure Group | Churn Rate |
|-------------|-----------|
| 0-1 Year | 47.68% |
| 1-2 Years | 28.71% |
| 2-4 Years | 20.39% |
| 4-6 Years | 9.51% |

### Insight

- New customers (0-1 Year) have the highest churn rate.
- Churn decreases as customer tenure increases.
- Customer retention programs should focus on the first year.

---

## Conclusion

- Gender has minimal effect on churn.
- Long-term contracts significantly reduce churn.
- Customer tenure is one of the strongest indicators of churn.
- Businesses should focus on retaining new customers and promoting long-term subscriptions.
