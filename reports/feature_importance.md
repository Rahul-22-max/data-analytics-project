# Feature Importance Report

## Top Features Influencing Customer Churn

| Feature | Coefficient | Impact |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Contract_One year | -0.6590 | Strongly reduces churn |
| PhoneService_Yes | -0.4815 | Reduces churn |
| OnlineSecurity_Yes | -0.4159 | Reduces churn |
| PaymentMethod_Electronic check | 0.4106 | Increases churn |
| TechSupport_Yes | -0.3839 | Reduces churn |
| PaperlessBilling_Yes | 0.3485 | Increases churn |
| MultipleLines_Yes | 0.2729 | Slightly increases churn |
| Dependents_Yes | -0.2410 | Reduces churn |

## Key Insights

### Features That Reduce Churn

- Customers with one-year contracts are significantly less likely to leave.
- Customers using Online Security services show stronger retention.
- Customers with Tech Support subscriptions have lower churn rates.
- Customers with dependents tend to remain customers longer.
- Customers with phone services show lower churn tendencies.

### Features That Increase Churn

- Customers paying through Electronic Check exhibit higher churn rates.
- Paperless billing is associated with increased churn.
- Customers with multiple phone lines show a slight tendency toward higher churn.

## Business Recommendations

1. Encourage customers to move to longer-term contracts.
2. Promote Online Security and Tech Support bundles.
3. Investigate churn patterns among Electronic Check users.
4. Improve retention campaigns for Paperless Billing customers.
5. Target high-risk customer segments with proactive offers.

## Conclusion

Contract duration, support services, and payment methods are the strongest drivers of customer churn in the current model. Long-term contracts and support-related services contribute positively to customer retention, while Electronic Check payments and Paperless Billing are associated with increased churn risk.
