# Prediction Analytics Report

## Objective

Analyze churn prediction outputs and segment customers according to churn risk.

## Risk Segmentation Results

| Category | Customers | Percentage |
|------------|----------:|----------:|
| High Risk | 504 | 7.16% |
| Medium Risk | 1579 | 22.42% |
| Low Risk | 4960 | 70.42% |

## Key Findings

- Most customers (70.42%) belong to the low-risk segment.
- 22.42% of customers require monitoring and engagement.
- 7.16% of customers are highly likely to churn and should be prioritized for retention campaigns.

## Business Recommendations

### High Risk Customers
- Immediate retention offers
- Personalized discounts
- Customer success outreach

### Medium Risk Customers
- Targeted marketing campaigns
- Service improvement communication
- Engagement programs

### Low Risk Customers
- Maintain service quality
- Cross-sell and upsell opportunities
